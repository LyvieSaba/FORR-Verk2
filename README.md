# Feed the Animals

A touch game for 2–3 year olds, made for phones and tablets. The game has no text, so everything is explained with animations.

## How to play
1. **Wake the animals:** the monkey and the fox are asleep. Hold a finger on an animal for 2 seconds. A yellow ring fills up around it and the animal wakes up.
2. **Open the jars:** put two fingers on a jar and twist, like opening a real jar. The lid spins off.
3. **Feed them:** a line shows which animal the food belongs to. Hold the food for a moment, then drag it to the right animal (banana to the monkey, fish to the fox). The animal eats it and gets happy. The wrong animal shakes its head and the food goes back.
4. **Level done:** when both animals have eaten, a star flies across the screen.
5. **Play again:** hold the round arrow button in the corner for 2 seconds.

## Made with
- **SVG** for all graphics 
- **anime.js v4.5** for animation, timeline, morphing, line drawing and motion path
